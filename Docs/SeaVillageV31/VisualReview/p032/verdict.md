- **Evidence/region/size:** Current B02, 1920 × 1080, approximately x930–1110 / y400–630 at the workshop porch and its adjacent straight dark bank; B04, 1920 × 1080, approximately x790–1075 / y405–760 at the tavern's long porch/base; B01's diagonal ground cut runs approximately from x258 / y790 toward x785 / y160. Nevertheless, the new porches still meet an abrupt dark linear gap/drop rather than a legible grounded apron or constructed terrace edge. Some exposed posts/end fragments sit along that line.
- **Inference boundary:** This could involve a retained terrain bank, a terrain join, lighting of a vertical face, placement, or some combination. Stills do not establish which mesh is faulty or prove a physics failure. The **visible integration defect** is established.
- **Consequence:** The houses do not convincingly occupy the same land surface as their approach. The hard local requirement explicitly does not exempt old seams when they affect new integration.
- **Required change:** Resolve the building/forecourt-to-bank composition as one local placement/contact problem: provide a continuous grounded forecourt or an intentional supported terrace with visible steps/retaining/support structure. Do not merely lower the foundation again while leaving the porch against a black cut.
- **Affected domains:** Workshop/tavern placement, adjacent land, approach readability.
- **Acceptance:** Repeat B02/B04 at native 1920 × 1080, showing the entire base, porch edge and immediate approach. No unexplained dark slit may run through/along the usable forecourt; every exposed deck edge must either meet ground visibly or have an intelligible supported drop and access. Also inspect the same contacts from a second oblique angle.
- **Status note:** The earlier excessive exposed-base and canopy-obstruction subproblems are visibly mitigated. HOUSE-01 is retained because the root building/terrain-contact requirement is still not satisfactorily evidenced; it is not declared fully resolved.
