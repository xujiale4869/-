# Construction grammar K

One metre units; ground-centred authored meshes, green vegetation with limited current sway. Marine roots follow the visible bed and visible tips remain under the water. Natural groupings use nonuniform scale/limited tilt instead of one identical repeated cluster. No new raster character/boat design.

Architecture: weathered brown timber with sparse broad beams, dark slate-coloured gable roofs, sea-stone low foundations, restrained warm windows, occupational silhouette props. Window frames/roof/ribs are geometry; shared existing wood and rock maps supply surface variation. The lookout has an octagonal shaft/tapered cap; tavern has a lower wing/chimney; workshop has open hull ribs/deck. New construction should sit beside existing nature with a clear forecourt rather than consume tree canopies.

Retained UI: dark cyan offset/skew plates, gold selection/diamonds, white line icons, Chinese labels, readable costs and separate resource pills. U01/U02 are 1080p/720p planning captures; absence of other states limits global conclusions. The UI identity is a fixed integration constraint.
